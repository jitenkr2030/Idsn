# 🏆 Annual All-India Digital Leader Competition - Implementation Complete

## ✅ **What's Been Implemented**

### **1. Complete Database Schema**
- **Competition Table**: Manages competition levels, timelines, and status
- **CompetitionSubmission Table**: Tracks school submissions with points and tiers
- **CompetitionActivity Table**: Records individual activities and evidence
- **CompetitionLeaderboard Table**: Auto-calculates rankings at all levels
- **CompetitionCertificate Table**: Generates QR-code verified certificates

### **2. Full API Infrastructure**
- **Competition Management API**: Create, update, and manage competitions
- **Submission API**: Handle school submissions with automatic point calculation
- **Activity API**: Add, update, and delete competition activities
- **Leaderboard API**: Real-time rankings at district, state, and national levels
- **Certificate API**: Generate and verify competition certificates
- **Automation API**: Automated notifications, approvals, and status updates

### **3. Principal Dashboard Components**
- **CompetitionDashboard**: Overview with stats and top performers
- **CompetitionManagement**: Full submission management for principals
- **Activity Management**: Add/edit activities with evidence upload
- **Progress Tracking**: Real-time points, tier, and rank display
- **Certificate Generation**: Auto-generate downloadable certificates

### **4. Automation System**
- **Deadline Reminders**: Automatic notifications 7 days before deadlines
- **Tier Upgrade Notifications**: Celebrate when schools reach new tiers
- **Auto-Approval System**: Automatically approve evidence based on criteria
- **Status Updates**: Automatic competition status transitions
- **Leaderboard Updates**: Real-time rank recalculations

### **5. Certificate & Verification System**
- **QR Code Generation**: Unique QR codes for each certificate
- **Verification Page**: Public verification page at `/certificates/[id]`
- **Download Functionality**: PDF certificate downloads
- **Social Sharing**: Share certificates on social media
- **Anti-Fraud**: Tamper-proof verification system
